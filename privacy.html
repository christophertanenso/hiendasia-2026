Privacy notice

HiEnd Asia 2026 website · Last updated 5 November 2026

This notice explains what personal data the HiEnd Asia 2026 website collects, why, and the choices you have. It is written for the Personal Data Protection Act 2012 of Singapore (PDPA) and applies to this website only. The show itself is free to enter and needs no registration.

Who we are

HiEnd Asia + Pte. Ltd, 1093 Lower Delta Road #02-09, Singapore 169204, organises the 2026 Singapore Hi-End Asia International Audio Visual Show and runs this website.

Data protection contact: michelle@hiendasia.com · +65 8668 1927. Write to us for any question, access or correction request, or complaint about your personal data.

What we collect

  • Nothing you type. The site has no forms, accounts or tickets. If you email, phone or message us, we use what you send only to reply.
  • Analytics, only if you allow it. Google Analytics records which pages and sections are viewed, which buttons are used (for example "Add to calendar", "Get directions", "Share"), the language, device and browser type, approximate location (city level) and how you arrived (for example a WhatsApp share or an ad).
  • Marketing, only if you allow it. The Meta Pixel and Google Ads tags record that you visited and the same actions, so we can measure our ads on Facebook, Instagram and Google and show HiEnd Asia ads to people who have visited. Meta and Google may link this to your account with them under their own policies.
  • QR signs at the show. If you open the site from a sign at the venue, the page notes which sign, so we can count visits on site.

Your choice and consent

Analytics and marketing tags stay off, and their scripts are not even downloaded, until you choose in the cookie banner. You can accept all, reject all, or choose each category. You can change or withdraw your choice at any time; withdrawing stops new collection and removes the analytics and marketing cookies this site set in your browser.

Who receives it, and where

Google LLC (Google Analytics, Google Ads, Google Fonts) and Meta Platforms Ireland Ltd (Meta Pixel) process this data for us, and may store it outside Singapore, including in the United States. We use them under their standard business terms, which include contractual safeguards for transferring personal data abroad. We do not sell personal data. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.), which keeps server logs, including IP addresses, for security.

Links to Google Calendar, Outlook, WhatsApp, WeChat and Google Maps take you to those services, and their own privacy policies apply there.

How long we keep it

Analytics data is kept for up to 14 months, then deleted. Ad data is kept by Meta and Google for the periods in their policies. Emails and messages are kept for as long as we need them to answer and to follow up on the show, then deleted.

Your rights

You can ask us for access to, or correction of, personal data we hold about you, and withdraw consent at any time, using the contact above. We will reply within 30 days. If you are not satisfied, you can contact the Personal Data Protection Commission at pdpc.gov.sg.

Changes

If we change this notice, we will update the date above. If a change affects what you agreed to, the cookie banner will ask you again.
