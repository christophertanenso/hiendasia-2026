Keynote Speakers

主题演讲嘉宾

Renowned experts and thought leaders in high-end audio share their insights in an intimate setting. Held in private rooms, these sessions go deep, from design philosophies to emerging technologies.

多位高端音响领域的权威专家将于专属空间内，深入分享他们的见解与经验。从设计理念到前沿技术，带您探讨音响艺术的更深层次。

Keynote · 主题演讲

Jack Liu

劉漢盛

Editor, Audio Art · Taiwan

Liu Hansheng has edited Audio Art, Taiwan's leading Hi-End audio magazine, since 1988. The monthly covers global audio equipment, tuning tips and new music, and is a key voice in the Chinese-speaking audio world.

Keynote · 主题演讲

Patrick Lee

Lee388

Hi-fi reviewer · Hong Kong

A Hong Kong hi-fi reviewer since the 1980s, Patrick shares decades of expertise through magazines, social media and YouTube. His Hi-Fi Circle community has over 40,000 members across Greater China and Southeast Asia.

Keynote · 主题演讲

Richard H. Mak

Analog Equipment Editor · Mono & Stereo

Analog editor for Mono & Stereo and Part Time Audiophile, and Analog Master at TONEAudio. A writer for 25 years and an audiophile for 44, he designed AnalogMagik, the world's only all-in-one cartridge setup software.

Keynote · 主题演讲

AdiLance

草爸

Audiophile and creator

A fellow audiophile who shares his journey into music and hi-fi on social media, in a characteristically frank manner that makes a refreshing change for those following along.

Keynote · 主题演讲

John Hsu

Room acoustics · Singapore

An audiophile for 45 years and a Singapore resident, John has built and tuned a dedicated basement listening room. His interactive session covers psychoacoustics, stereo imaging, room treatment and speaker placement.

HiEnd Asia 精选多位国际和亚洲艺术家登上主舞台，呈现多语言、多文化交融的音乐风貌。从声学纯净到文化细腻，每一场演出，皆是对聆听之美的深情致敬。

Why HiEnd Asia

为何选择 HiEnd Asia

  1. 2023Sofitel SingaporeDebut edition · 6,000 visits
  2. 2024Pan Pacific SingaporeExpanded showcase · 8,000+ patrons
  3. 2025Suntec SingaporeFirst edition at a world-class convention venue
  4. 2026Suntec Singapore, Level 317 – 19 December

HiEnd Asia has quickly become Southeast Asia's most anticipated high-end audio exhibition. At Suntec Singapore Convention & Exhibition Centre, at the heart of the city, it brings more exhibitors, more brands and purpose-built listening rooms, just minutes from three MRT lines.

As Southeast Asia moves towards becoming half of the global economy by 2030, demand for high-end, lifestyle audio is accelerating. Singapore, a gateway to over 4 billion people within a 7-hour flight, offers global access, pro-business infrastructure and Asian elegance.

自2023年首展于 Sofitel 酒店圆满落幕，三日吸引六千人次；到 2024 年移师 Pan Pacific 酒店，迎来超过八千参观人次。HiEnd Asia 已迅速成为东南亚最受瞩目的高端音响展览之一。

东南亚正以惊人速度增长，预计2030年将占据全球经济的一半。新加坡在七小时飞行半径内连接超过40亿人口，凭借其国际通达力、亲商环境与独特亚洲魅力，成为 HiEnd Asia 理想之选。
